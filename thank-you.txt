You read all of it. Not skimmed, not scored in a hurry — read, the way a person reads a letter that matters. Somewhere in the pile was a line someone stayed up late to get right, and you gave it the one thing it was asking for: your full attention, for as long as it took. That is the whole job, and you did it. When you are done tonight, I hope the room is quiet, the lamp is still warm, and the chair you pulled out for the next reader is still waiting — because somebody, somewhere, is already writing the next one, trusting you will read it too.
